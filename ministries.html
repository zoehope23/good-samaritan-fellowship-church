Ministry for Every Season of Life

From children to seniors, from new believers to seasoned followers — our ministries are designed to help you grow in faith and community.

🧒

Children's Ministry

Our Children's Ministry provides a safe, fun, and faith-filled environment for young ones to learn about Jesus. Through Bible stories, songs, and activities, children are nurtured in their faith from an early age.

For children aged 3–12
🔥

Youth Ministry

The Youth Ministry is a vibrant community for teenagers and young adults to encounter God, build lasting friendships, and discover their purpose. Events, Bible studies, and worship nights are core to youth life at GSF.

For ages 13–25
🎵

Worship Ministry

Our worship team leads the congregation into God's presence through music and song. If you have a gift for music, singing, or sound — there's a place for you to serve and grow in the Worship Ministry.

For all ages
🌸

Women's Fellowship

A space for women to gather, pray, study Scripture, and encourage one another. The Women's Fellowship builds strong sisterhood relationships and helps women grow in faith, confidence, and purpose.

For women of all ages
💪

Men's Fellowship

Men gather to study the Word, pray together, and build each other up in faith. Our Men's Fellowship helps men become Godly leaders in their homes, workplaces, and communities.

For men of all ages
🙏

Prayer Ministry

Prayer is foundational at GSF. The Prayer Ministry coordinates intercession, prayer meetings, and individual prayer support. We believe in a God who answers prayer and moves on behalf of His people.

For all members
🤝

Outreach & Social Ministry

Living out the Good Samaritan calling, we actively serve our surrounding community through outreach programmes, compassion work, and social initiatives — bringing hope to those in need.

For all members
👴

Seniors' Fellowship

We honour and cherish our senior members. The Seniors' Fellowship provides fellowship, pastoral care, and meaningful engagement for those in the golden years of life.

For seniors 60+
💍

Marriage & Family Ministry

Strong families build strong churches. Our Marriage and Family Ministry offers counsel, enrichment events, and support for couples and families at every stage of life.

For couples & families

3

4

Grow
