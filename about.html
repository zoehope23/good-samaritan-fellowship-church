Our Story

Who We Are

Good Samaritan Fellowship began with a simple calling: to be a light in the community and a home for every seeking heart.

Good Samaritan Fellowship (GSF) is an English-speaking, Spirit-filled congregation located at Hosanna Tower in Kodungaiyur, Chennai — the English Church near Kannadasan Nagar. Founded on the principles of love, hope, and faith, our church has grown into a vibrant community of believers from diverse backgrounds, united by their shared faith in Jesus Christ.

We believe in the power of prayer, the authority of God's Word, and the transforming grace of the Holy Spirit. Our vision is to see every person in our city encounter the living God — and our mission is to make that happen through worship, teaching, and compassionate service.

A Life Devoted to God's Calling

Founder | Good Samaritan Fellowship, Chennai

Rev. A. Owen Robert is the founding pastor of Good Samaritan Fellowship, a servant of God whose life has been marked by faithful dedication, prayerful devotion, and a deep love for people. Over many years of ministry, he has steadfastly built this congregation from its earliest days into a thriving community of faith.

His ministry has been characterized by a sincere commitment to the Word of God and a heart for the spiritually hungry. Through consistent preaching, pastoral care, and community engagement, Rev. Owen Robert has shaped the spiritual identity of Good Samaritan Fellowship.

God has worked powerfully through his ministry, touching and transforming many lives in Chennai and beyond. His legacy is not measured in numbers alone, but in the countless lives that have been renewed, restored, and rooted in faith through his steadfast service.

Rev. Owen Robert's vision — to offer love, hope, and faith to every person — continues to guide everything we do at Good Samaritan Fellowship.
